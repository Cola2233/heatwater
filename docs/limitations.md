# Interpretation and research limits

The water layer is a terrain/hardening/land-cover teaching proxy. It has not
been calibrated against observed flood locations, drainage capacity, rainfall
return periods or flood depth. Its score supports candidate-area screening,
not flood prediction or engineering certification.

LST is land-surface temperature, not 2 m air temperature or personal heat
exposure. Two composite windows cannot establish an annual trend. Separate
normalization emphasizes relative spatial patterns and removes absolute
temperature differences between the windows.

Building functions locate potential service/residential exposure. They do not
measure census population, age, occupancy or vulnerability. A building polygon
touching a pixel marks presence, not fractional built area or occupant density.

Weight and threshold sensitivity describes parameter dependence; it does not
replace external validation. To strengthen scientific validity, obtain
independent flood observations, population/occupancy data, rainfall and drainage
data; specify matching time windows and spatial support; then evaluate held-out
locations and uncertainty. No such performance claim is made by version 0.1.0.

Prepared local inputs have incomplete acquisition and licensing records.
Real-data redistribution is pending that review; the generated demo is fully
available and explicitly marked synthetic.
